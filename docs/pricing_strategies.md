# Pricing Strategies

- **Cost-based:** price = cost + markup. Simple, but can ignore customer value.
- **Value-based:** price around perceived customer value. Best fit for LearnFlow Pro.
- **Competitor-based:** anchor to comparable products. Useful as a guardrail.
- **Penetration:** lower initial price to accelerate adoption.
- **Premium:** higher price supported by differentiated value.
- **Freemium:** free entry with paid upgrades.
- **Subscription:** recurring monthly/annual payment.
- **Tiered:** packages for different needs.
- **Psychological:** e.g., ₹1,199 instead of ₹1,200.
- **Dynamic:** price varies with demand, segment, or context.
