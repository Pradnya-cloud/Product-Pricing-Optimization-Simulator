# A/B Pricing Experiment

**Control:** ₹999. **Test:** ₹1,199.

Randomly assign eligible new visitors 50/50. Keep features, acquisition channel, messaging, and billing terms constant. Track conversion, revenue per visitor, churn, ARPU, LTV and profit. Use a pre-defined decision rule: launch only if the test improves profit per visitor and does not cause a material churn increase, while the conversion decline remains commercially acceptable.
