# Business Insights

1. ₹1,199 is the recommended core price in the base model.
2. The highest price does not maximize profit because demand and conversion fall.
3. ₹999 is the current anchor and is useful for the A/B control group.
4. Students are the most price-sensitive segment.
5. Enterprise has the highest willingness to pay and lowest modeled price sensitivity.
6. Blanket discounts reduce realized revenue per customer; targeted discounts are safer.
7. Small Business is a strong upsell segment because WTP is materially above the entry price.
8. Margin rises as price increases when unit cost stays stable.
9. LTV:CAC should be monitored alongside conversion rather than using revenue alone.
10. A/B testing should validate conversion, revenue/visitor, churn, ARPU, LTV, and profit before rollout.
